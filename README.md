# WANANO

WANANO is an AI-assisted relationship and marriage matchmaking platform designed around verified identities, thoughtful compatibility and meaningful connections.

## Product principles
- Adults aged 18+ only.
- Identity verification before matchmaking access.
- Live-video liveness checks through a suitable verification provider.
- Facebook account connection and account-history assessment, without claiming unsupported access to account creation dates.
- Mutual acceptance creates one exclusive matchmaking connection at a time.
- Either member can end or block an exclusive connection; safety release is immediate.
- Sensitive identity documents and biometric information must never be publicly accessible.

## Current status
Stage 1 frontend foundation is being prepared. Authentication, database, identity verification, social verification, matchmaking, messaging and payments are not active yet.

## Next stages
1. Supabase authentication and database.
2. Member profiles and preferences with Row Level Security.
3. Verification-provider integration and secure verification records.
4. Compatibility and exclusive-match engine.
5. Messaging, moderation, subscriptions and payments.

Do not collect real identity documents or biometric data until the verification provider, consent flow, retention policy and access controls have been implemented and tested.
